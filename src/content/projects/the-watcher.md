# The Lawyer

*The Lawyer* is a first-person investigative horror game built with Unreal Engine 5. Designed as a one-month project to explore immersive escape room mechanics using the Horror Engine plugin, the game drops players into a seemingly normal vintage law office... and locks the door behind them.

## Overview

You wake up alone, disoriented, and the silence is deafening. There's no tutorial—only the subtle creaks of wooden floors and a desk lamp that flickers without rhythm. The room breathes secrets, and something about it feels... *wrong*.

## Gameplay

Players must uncover the twisted secrets of the lawyer’s past by solving puzzles, collecting hidden clues, and opening secret passageways. As the game progresses, the true horror of the office is slowly revealed.

- **Hidden Levers & Secret Rooms**: pull books to shift shelves, activate wall switches buried in shadow, and uncover hidden trapdoors.
- **Clues Written in Blood**: discover unsettling codes and messages scrawled in red, hinting at what really went on in this office.
- **Atmospheric Horror**: tension builds through precise sound design, dynamic lighting, and sudden but carefully placed jumpscares.
- **Handmade & Free Assets**: the game blends free online resources with custom-built elements such as documents, notes, and interaction items for a personal touch.

## Narrative

Behind the polished facade of law and order lies a man with a monstrous secret. Through found notes, locked drawers, and hidden chambers, players piece together the disturbing truth: the lawyer wasn’t just working late—he was imprisoning and torturing victims in a secret room beneath the office.

## Tools & Tech

The game was developed using **Unreal Engine 5**, with key systems built through **Blueprint** and enhanced by the **Horror Engine** toolkit. The modular design allows for potential expansion with new rooms, puzzles, and narrative branches.
