# Bedtime Nightmare

*Bedtime Nightmare* is a first-person psychological horror game developed by **Unseen Cat Studio**. Light is your only protection: complete simple nightly tasks while surviving in the dark, knowing that every action drains the safety keeping you alive.

## Overview

You wake up in the middle of the night. Your room is too dark, but the mess will not let you sleep. Familiar routines become survival tasks as the lights begin to fail and something starts moving in the darkness.

The experience is grounded in childhood anxieties and compulsions. Ordinary domestic spaces become hostile once the lights go out, turning small actions into tense decisions about time, safety, and risk.

## Core Experience

Each night asks the player to balance routine with survival:

- **Keep the lights on** — darkness leaves you exposed.
- **Complete your tasks** — finish what must be done before your safety runs out.
- **Restart the generator** — restore power when the house goes dark.
- **Watch for anomalies** — familiar rooms may not stay familiar.
- **Uncover the truth** — piece together what is happening inside the house.

The result is a challenging, tense, replayable, and nostalgic horror experience. Players must learn when to move, when to recharge, and when the darkness is no longer empty.

## Story

You are a child left alone at night. Simple routines become survival tasks, shaped by childhood fears, anxieties, and compulsions. When the lights go out, something begins moving through the house.

## Visual Direction

The game is experienced from a low, child-height perspective. Distorted scale makes ordinary furniture and household objects feel imposing.

Light and darkness drive both the visual language and the gameplay. High-contrast rooms rely on shadow and silhouette as much as clearly visible threats. The environments draw from nostalgic early-2000s domestic spaces: lived-in, familiar, and quietly wrong once night falls.

## Design Focus

My work focused on translating the central fantasy — **light means safety** — into a readable and repeatable gameplay loop. Tasks create forward pressure, while limited battery and generator interruptions force the player to leave safe areas and make deliberate risk-versus-reward decisions.

The project was developed through continuous prototyping, playtesting, and iteration, with particular attention to tension curves, environmental guidance, task clarity, and the relationship between familiar routines and horror escalation.

## Release

*Bedtime Nightmare* was released on Steam on **June 23, 2026**, with support for English and Italian.
