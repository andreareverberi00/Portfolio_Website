# Lost Balance

Lost Balance is a survival horror game that revolves around maintaining equilibrium on three emotional axes: anxiety/calm, fear/rashness, and depression/euphoria.

## Core Mechanics

Players navigate these axes in response to various environmental factors and objects, balancing their emotions to prevent hitting an extreme, which would lead to game over.

## Narrative Design

The game explores themes of mental health and emotional regulation through a horror lens, creating a unique gameplay experience where your emotional state directly impacts your survival.
