## Overview

Space Pizza is an interactive pixel art game set in a thrilling cyberpunk universe. The player takes on the role of an astronaut stranded on a distant planet following a space accident. In a bid to gather enough funds to repair their spaceship and return home, they start working at an interstellar diner, preparing and delivering pizzas to various alien customers.

## Gameplay

The core gameplay loop revolves around the following steps:
1. **Starting the Workday**: Players begin their day by receiving orders from customers.
2. **Order Management**: Whenever a customer approaches the counter, a thought bubble appears showing the specific pizza ingredients needed, along with a timer for delivery. Failing to deliver on time results in monetary penalties.
3. **Pizza Preparation**: Players gather ingredients from a pool, ensuring to discard any undesirable items to make space for the right ones. Players must mix ingredients on a table to make the pizza and bake it before delivering it down the counter.
4. **Client Feedback**: Upon successful delivery of the correct order within the time limit, players earn money. Any mistakes lead to theft by the customer and negative reviews.

## Game Mechanics

- **Ingredient Management**: Players must manage a variety of good and bad ingredients while preparing pizzas. Good ingredients include tomatoes, mozzarella, and salami, while bad ones can include alien tentacles and cyborg eyes.
- **Customer Interaction**: Players must pay attention to each order’s timing and correctness, affecting their reputation and finances based on customer feedback.
- **Reputation System**: Client reviews directly impact the player's income, with negative reviews resulting in decreased sales and potential dismissal from the job.

## Narrative

In this light-hearted narrative, players embody an astronaut who finds themselves in a pickle after a misfortune in space. To repair their ship and return home, witty dialogues and storylines framed in a graphic novel style enhance the experience, providing both humor and depth to the game.

## Visual Style

Space Pizza features vibrant pixel art graphics, set against a cyberpunk backdrop that immerses players in a futuristic diner environment. The user interface draws inspiration from management simulators, enhancing the gaming experience.

## Sound Design

As part of my responsibilities, I contributed to the game’s soundtrack, crafting music that complements the fast-paced environment of the space diner while enhancing player engagement.
