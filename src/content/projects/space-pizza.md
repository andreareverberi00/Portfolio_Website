## Overview

Space Pizza is a unique pixel art game that combines the excitement of space exploration with the fast-paced demands of managing a pizza shop. Players assume the role of an astronaut stranded on a distant planet, who must work at a space diner to earn enough money to repair their spaceship. The game blends elements of strategy, time management, and narrative-driven gameplay.

## Gameplay Core Loop

Players start their workday by receiving pizza orders from customers. Each order includes specific ingredients, and players must manage their time effectively to prepare and deliver the pizzas before time runs out. Failure to deliver on time results in penalties from impatient customers, while delivering the correct pizza earns rewards. The core gameplay loop involves receiving orders, preparing pizzas with good ingredients while avoiding bad ones, and ensuring timely deliveries.

## Game Mechanics

- **Ingredient Management**: Players must navigate a pool of ingredients, disposing of bad ones and saving those needed for correct orders. 
- **Interactive Cooking**: Interaction with various props allows players to prepare pizzas by rolling out dough and choosing toppings. 
- **Customer Interaction**: Customers provide visual hints about their orders and impose time constraints that affect gameplay.

## Narrative Elements

The game's story unfolds through graphic novel-style dialogues, providing depth to the astronaut's struggles and relationships with extraterrestrial customers. The narrative interweaves humor with the challenges of working in a busy space diner.

## Visual Style

Space Pizza features charming pixel art graphics set against a vibrant cyberpunk backdrop, creating an immersive atmosphere that reflects the quirky yet challenging life of a pizza delivery worker in space.

## Conclusion

Space Pizza combines engaging gameplay with a rich narrative and a distinct visual aesthetic, offering players a fun and unique experience as they navigate the challenges of intergalactic pizza delivery.
