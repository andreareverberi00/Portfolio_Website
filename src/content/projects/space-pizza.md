## Overview

Space Pizza is a pixel art game set in a vibrant cyberpunk space environment. Players take on the role of an astronaut stranded on a planet after a space accident. To gather funds needed to repair their ship and return home, they work in a space diner, preparing and delivering pizzas to various alien customers.

## Gameplay Core Loop

Players start their workday by receiving pizza orders from customers. Each customer specifies ingredients for their pizza, triggering a countdown for delivery. If the pizza is not delivered on time, players lose money. The gameplay involves collecting ingredients, preparing pizzas, and ensuring timely delivery to satisfy customers and earn tips.

## Game Mechanics

The game features a unique ingredient management system where players must sort through a pool of ingredients, discarding unwanted items to make room for necessary ones. Key mechanics include:

- Interactions with ingredients to assemble pizzas.
- Cooking pizzas and delivering them to customers within the set time.
- Receiving feedback from customers through a review system that impacts overall gameplay and financial rewards.

## Narrative

The narrative follows the astronaut's struggles after a space accident, combining gameplay with a graphic novel style dialogue system. The storyline unfolds as players progress, deepening the player's connection with the protagonist and their plight.

## Visual Style

Space Pizza showcases a charming pixel art graphic style, reflecting a bustling cyberpunk diner setting. The interface incorporates elements inspired by management simulation games, enhancing the gameplay experience.

## Key Ingredients

- **Good Ingredients:** Tomato, Mozzarella, Olive, Basil, Salami.
- **Bad Ingredients:** Alien Tentacle, Gear, Slime, Cyborg Eye, Space Insect.

## Customer Interactions

Players deal with a variety of customers, each with their own personalities and urgency levels. Timely and correct deliveries result in positive reviews, whereas mistakes lead to financial penalties and negative feedback.
